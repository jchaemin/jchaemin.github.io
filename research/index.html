Research

My research looks at language models from three sides. From the inside: what a model has learned, how that knowledge is stored and retrieved, and what stops it from being used. From the outside: how faithfully models can stand in for people, as survey respondents, personas, and participants in public deliberation. And in the field: how models can measure places where ground-truth data is scarce.
