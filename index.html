Bio

I am an M.S. student in the School of Computing at KAIST, co-advised by Jihee Kim and Dongman Lee.

I am interested in the behavior of large language models — what they represent, what they actually do with it, and why the two come apart. I work by measuring behavior on tasks where the correct answer is known in advance, then asking which training choices and internal mechanisms produce the gap. A few directions I keep returning to: how models arbitrate between knowledge and context; what alignment training changes about a model's output distribution; and when models can serve as proxies for people, and when they cannot.
