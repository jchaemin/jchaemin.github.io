I study what language models know and how they use it.

I’m Chaemin Jang, an M.S. student in the School of Computing at KAIST, co-advised by Jihee Kim and Dongman Lee. My work examines factual recall, internal judgments, and why a model’s knowledge does not always appear in its outputs.

Previously, I worked on machine learning research and engineering at KAIST, IBS, and NSUSLAB.
